I am a postdoctoral researcher in the [Automated Reasoning Group](https://arg.cs.uni-kl.de/en/), led by [Anthony W. Lin](https://anthonywlin.github.io/), at RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau, Germany.

I completed my PhD at the University of Kassel in 2025. My dissertation is
[The Complexity of Formal Verification of Neural Network Based Models](https://kobra.uni-kassel.de/items/378d2c97-5d29-48ef-9087-1f2c59eacd59).

## Research

My research connects formal methods and machine learning. I study what neural networks can express,
which properties can be verified algorithmically, and the computational complexity of these tasks.
My work covers feedforward neural networks, graph neural networks, and transformers.

[Browse my publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).
