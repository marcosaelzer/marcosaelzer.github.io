We introduce a logic for quantized graph neural networks that combine local aggregation with global readout. It gives a precise way to reason about these models and establishes (co)NEXPTIME-completeness for verification tasks. Experiments also examine how quantization affects model size, accuracy, and generalization.

An earlier preprint was titled *Verifying Graph Neural Networks with Readout is Intractable*.
