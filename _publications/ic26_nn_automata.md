We translate the input-output behavior of deep neural networks into weak Büchi automata. This representation lets us apply automata-based methods to verification and interpretation questions, including adversarial robustness and minimum sufficient reasons.

The journal article appeared in *Information and Computation*, volume 308, January 2026. An earlier conference version appeared at [DLT 2024](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-66159-4_19), pages 266–281.
